Mon Studio — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026
Mon Studio is a personal, owner-operated content dashboard used exclusively by the developer for their own TikTok account.

1. Terms of Service

  • Who may use it: This application is personal and single-user. It is operated by its developer for the developer's own TikTok account only. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to third parties.
  • Permitted use: The app reads the account owner's own public statistics and video list, and publishes videos manually initiated by the account owner. All posted content is owned by the account owner.
  • No third-party access: The app never accesses, stores, or displays content belonging to other users. No automated mass posting, no scraping, no data resale.
  • Compliance: Use of the TikTok Developer Platform is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Developer Platform Terms, and the applicable API terms. If the app's use conflicts with TikTok's rules, TikTok's rules prevail.
  • No warranty / liability: The app is provided "as is". The developer is the sole user and accepts full responsibility for the content they publish through it.

2. Privacy Policy

  • Data processed: The app processes only (a) the OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by TikTok for the account owner's own account, and (b) the account owner's own public data (username, profile info, video list and basic statistics) fetched for the dashboard.
  • Purpose: Solely to display the account owner's own analytics and to publish videos the owner manually uploads through the dashboard.
  • Storage: Tokens are stored locally, encrypted-at-rest, on the developer's own machine, for the sole purpose of authenticating the account owner's own session. No data is stored on third-party servers.
  • Sharing: No personal data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the dashboard for its owner.
  • Retention & deletion: Tokens are kept only while the dashboard is in use by the owner and can be deleted at any time by revoking the app in TikTok account settings (Settings → Privacy → Manage app activity) or by contacting the developer (the account owner themselves).
  • Contact: For any privacy question, contact the developer/owner of the application — since the app is personal, the data subject and the data controller are the same person.
